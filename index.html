1. Overview

Dr. Sungworld ("we," "us," or "our") provides the mobile game Retro Brick Breaker (package name: com.drsungworld.retrobreaker). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

The game does not require account registration and does not use a developer-operated server. We do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Data stored locally on your device

To preserve game progress and preferences, the game stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Your selected language
  • Best score and best survival time
  • Current life count and the UTC timestamp used to calculate life recharge

This local game data is not transmitted to us. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

3. Rewarded advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The game offers optional rewarded advertisements, such as watching an ad to continue a run or receive an additional life. You may choose not to watch a rewarded ad.

We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). According to Google's SDK disclosure, Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • App interactions, including app launches, taps, and ad or video views
  • Diagnostic information related to app and SDK performance
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available

Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or used only for technical delivery depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by law, the game uses UMP to request or update advertising consent before requesting ads. You can make choices through the consent form when it is presented. You can also manage Google advertising preferences through My Ad Center and your Android privacy settings.

Clearing the game's app storage resets locally stored game settings and may cause the consent message to be requested again.

5. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Google through the advertising SDK as described above. We do not provide personal information to other third parties through a developer-operated server because the game does not operate such a server.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. We do not maintain an account database or server-side gameplay profile to delete. Data processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and your Google or device settings.

7. Children's privacy

Retro Brick Breaker is a general-audience arcade game and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the game, please contact us.

8. Security

We limit the game's own data storage to the information needed for local progress and preferences. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures and reputable platform services appropriate to the game.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when game features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
